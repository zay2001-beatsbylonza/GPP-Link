THANK YOU
=========

Thank you for downloading GPP Link.

I made GPP Link so my beats could move between my MPCs and my computer
without getting in the way of the music. Pick a beat, play it on the
cassette, press send, and get back to making music. I hope it does the
same for you.

GPP Link is free and open source. If it helps you, share it with a
producer friend.

COMING SOON: GET PRODUCERS PAID ON THE APP STORE
Get Producers Paid is my iPhone app for producers to sell their beats.
GPP Link is built to work with it. The SEND TO GET PRODUCERS PAID and
SEND STEMS TO GET PRODUCERS PAID buttons already collect your finished
beats and stem sets in the Get Producers Paid folder on your Mac. When
the app launches, GPP Link will send them straight to your producer
account as drafts. You add the title, price and license, publish, and
get paid, from MPC to sale without retyping or re-uploading anything.

Look out for the Apple App Store launch.

GETTING STARTED
  - Quick Start: GPP-Link-Quick-Start.pdf (one page)
  - Full guide with pictures: GPP-Link-How-To-Guide.pdf
  - Remember: turn on Wi-Fi on your MPC (Menu > Preferences > Wi-Fi) and
    keep the GPP Link hub window open on your Mac.
