THANK YOU
=========

Thank you for downloading GPP Link.

I made GPP Link so my beats could move between my MPCs and my computer
without getting in the way of the music. Pick a beat, play it on the
cassette, press send, and get back to making music. I hope it does the
same for you.

GPP Link is free and open source. If it helps you, share it with a
producer friend.

GETTING STARTED
  - Quick Start: GPP-Link-Quick-Start.pdf (one page)
  - Full guide with pictures: GPP-Link-How-To-Guide.pdf
  - Remember: turn on Wi-Fi on your MPC (Menu > Preferences > Wi-Fi) and
    keep the GPP Link hub window open on your Mac.
