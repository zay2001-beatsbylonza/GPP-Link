THANK YOU
=========

Thank you for downloading GPP Link.

I made GPP Link so my beats could move between my MPCs and my computer
without getting in the way of the music. Pick a beat, play it on the
cassette, press send, and get back to making music. I hope it does the
same for you.

GPP Link is free and open source. If it helps you, share it with a
producer friend.

GET PRODUCERS PAID
Get Producers Paid is my iPhone app for producers to sell their beats,
coming soon to the App Store. GPP Link is built to work with it: beats and
stem sets you send with the SEND TO GET PRODUCERS PAID buttons land in the
Get Producers Paid folder on your computer. Connect your store once on
the hub page (open http://localhost:47800 on that computer and type the
pairing code from the app), and GPP Link uploads each one to your
producer account as a draft. You add the title, price and license,
publish, and get paid, from MPC to sale without re-uploading anything.

GETTING STARTED
  - Quick Start: GPP-Link-Quick-Start.pdf (one page)
  - Full guide with pictures: GPP-Link-How-To-Guide.pdf
  - Remember: turn on Wi-Fi on your MPC (Menu > Preferences > Wi-Fi) and
    keep the GPP Link hub window open on your computer.
